## 2. What landed

- **Gradient tray scrollbar thumb** (`puzzle-board.tsx` + palette tokens): the
  draggable pill no longer paints itself white with a grass stroke. It now fills
  with a `LinearGradient` reading the active theme's new palette pair — light on
  the left → deep on the right — drawn in the thumb group's _local_ coordinates
  (x `0 → trayThumbW`), so the direction stays LEFT→RIGHT wherever the pill sits
  on the track. The rounded highlight shadow and outline stay
  `rgba(58,43,26,0.25)`; the three grip lines recoloured from `grass` (invisible
  on the pale end of the gradient) to `onFill`/white at 0.75 opacity, which
  holds on both the light left and the deep right. Track and arrow buttons are
  byte-for-byte the ci-22 versions.
- **New palette tokens** (`tokens.ts`): `trayScrollbarStart: '#A4FF7D'`,
  `trayScrollbarEnd: '#0A8B24'` in the shared palette, so every theme must
  supply the pair. **WOOD overrides** (`themes.ts`): `#C19A6C → #BA8A65` — the
  thumb reads as warm oak on the desk, grass on the meadow, with no per-theme
  code in the component.
- **8pt board frame inset** (`tray-geometry.ts` + `game-screen.tsx`): new
  `BOARD_FRAME_PAD = 8` screen points (pinned to `spacing.sm`), applied as the
  board shell's own `padding`. Because the `PuzzleBoard` canvas onLayout measures
  the shell's _padded_ inner box, the mat, tray shelf, scrollbar track/thumb and
  every drag/flick/tap gesture automatically respect the inset at all 13 grid
  sizes (9 → 784 pieces) — the board corners now read as a picture inside a mat
  instead of crowding the shell's rounded corners. `maxHeight` and all existing
  geometry formulas are unchanged.

## 3. Design notes

- The gradient lives entirely in the palette layer, keeping
  `themes.test.ts`'s no-hardcoded-hex rule intact (component source only sees
  theme tokens and `rgba(...)`).
- The thumb's gradient coordinates are fixed in the group's local space rather
  than the canvas, so sliding the pill never skews the gradient direction or
  hops its stops.
- One `BOARD_FRAME_PAD` value does the work of a per-grid layout pass; the
  scrollbar's outer-reach hit areas and the tray shelf simply land 8pt inside
  the shell on every device and density.
