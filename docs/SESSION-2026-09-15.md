### 2.1 Post-merge regression caught by tests

The first full run after merging failed exactly 2 treasure-screen tests:
"shows the day, stop count and difficulty tier" expected `Day 1 — stop 1 of 7 ·
Easy` but rendered `Start the trail` (streak 0), and the reward test found the
right title but `recordOnce` never fired.

**Root cause:** the test helper built day keys with `date.toISOString().slice
(0,10)` (UTC), while the app's `dateKey`/`streakFrom` (`src/data/daily.ts`) use
**local** midnight. The suite was green exactly while UTC and local agreed on
"today"; once local midnight crossed (and after the merge's timing), a
`dayKey(-1)` completion bucketed two local days back, so `streakFrom` walked
over an empty day and the payment effect never re-ran. Fix (`741f94e`): the
test `dayKey()` now uses the app's own `dateKey(new Date() + offset days)` —
local, deterministic on every device/timezone, and `12:00Z` completedAt stamps
bucket to the same local day in every `±12h` timezone. No product code changed.

### 5.2 Corner snap bug — fix attempted, still reproduces on-device

**Original report (§5.2 in prior session):** after mis-dropping a piece at/near
a corner, the correct corner piece **no longer snaps** when placed aligned — no
"clunk", stays loose. Investigation found: snap is center-distance only vs the
piece's OWN solved slot; a wrong piece never "locks" at a foreign corner; loose
pieces render **after** locked clusters (`puzzle-board.tsx` ~1787/1798), so a
loose miss always draws over anything that then locks beneath it, and hit-testing
(`looseBoxes` topmost-first, ~1443) makes each new grab at that corner pick up
the covering loose piece instead — reads as "the correct piece won't fit again."
`clampPieceToBoard` (`tray-geometry.ts:234`) pins overhanging drops at the
corner (position → (0,0)), which parks misses exactly on the corner slot.

**Fix attempted (`143e955`):** after a successful snap, `dropPiece` now evicts
loose pieces that overlap the freshly locked slot back to the tray
(`position.y = boardHeight`, same mechanism as a tray-drop), clearing the
covering piece so the correct piece shows and stays grabbable. Extended
`DropPieceInput` with optional `boundsById` + `boardHeight`; `boxesIntersect`
checks AABB overlap; `buildNextSession` refactored out of `withUpdatedPiece`.

**On-device result: bug still reproduces.** The unit tests pass (9 eviction
tests covering all 4 corners + centre + guards, 506 total), but the physical
Pan gesture on the OnePlus CPH2487 still shows the correct piece not snapping
after a mis-drop covers its slot. **Root cause not yet identified** — possible
candidates:

- `releaseGeometry` may not include entries for tray pieces, so `boundsById`
  could be incomplete (missing pieces not currently rendered on the board).
- The `piece.position.y < boardHeight` guard may filter out pieces that are
  visually on the board but whose computed position exceeds `boardSize.height`
  due to coordinate-space differences.
- The AABB intersection may be too strict (touching edges don't count) or the
  `boardHeight` parameter may not match the actual board boundary used by the
  tray layout.

**Also reported:** a similar mis-drop covering a **middle (non-corner) slot**
exhibits the same failure — the correct piece doesn't snap when placed over a
covering loose piece in the centre of the board.

### 5.3 Root cause found and fixed (`58a6e43`)

The bug was **not** in the eviction logic — it was in the **snap check ordering**
in `puzzle-board.tsx` `releasePiece`. Edge/corner pieces have jigsaw tabs that
extend past the board boundary at their solved position (e.g. piece `3:0` on a
4×4 grid: solved.y=216, piece height=92.9, board=288 → extends 20.9 px below
the board).

`clampPieceToBoard` was called **before** `isWithinSnapDistance`. The clamp
pushed the piece's position away from the solved slot (max Y = 288−92.9 = 195.1
vs solved Y = 216), making `dist = 20.9` which **just exceeds** the snap
threshold of 20.2 (0.28 × 72 cell size).

Debug logging confirmed: piece `3:0` had `clamped.y=195.1`, `solved.y=216`,
`dist=20.9`, `thresh=20.2` → `snap=false` on every attempt.

**Fix:** check snap on the **raw** (unclamped) position first. Only fall back
to the clamped position for non-snapping drops. The snap path passes the raw
position to `dropPiece` (session.ts), which also checks snap distance and sets
the position to `solvedPosition` when snapping.

CI build **ci-47** (run `34958587529`, commit `58a6e43`) built, installed on
device (`d6edd1ce`). **On-device verification passed** — edge/corner pieces now
snap correctly when dragged to their solved positions.
